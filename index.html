Toowong Village Intersection Experience Survey

Anonymous Survey | Estimated completion time: 2–3 minutes

Purpose of the Survey

This short survey aims to understand people's experiences with traffic lights and intersections around Toowong Village. We are interested in identifying whether there are common issues with intersection waiting times and traffic conditions in this area.

Participant Information

Participation in this survey is voluntary. Please answer the questions based on your own experiences during the past month.

There are no right or wrong answers. You may skip any question that you do not wish to answer.

Anonymous Survey

This survey does not ask for your name, email address, or other direct identifying information. Please avoid entering personal information in any free-text response.
